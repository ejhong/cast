THE EXPLANATORY IDEA

What could this
make easier?

A workable mass settles between irregular neighbors. Timber and cloth support the exposed face as the new block takes the shape of its surroundings.

01

Close-fitting, irregular joints

A workable block can take an impression of neighboring surfaces instead of being repeatedly lifted for fitting.

Still to explainShrinkage and release still matter. Separate, tight-fitting blocks must remain separate after curing.

02

Putting a block into a confined gap

Material can arrive in small batches and acquire its final shape inside the wall.

Still to explainThis changes the size of each load, not the total mass to deliver or the need for access and support.

FROM POSSIBILITY TO EVIDENCE

How would
we tell?

The proposal

This is a candidate construction sequence inspired by the broader cast-stone proposal. The cloth arrangement and deformation are illustrative design choices, not recovered Inca equipment.

The comparison

Careful dressing, trial fitting, and abrasion provide a conventional account of polygonal masonry. A close joint by itself does not discriminate between these processes.

A USEFUL PHYSICAL TEST

Build a small wall using a specified putty-like mixture. Measure slump, joint gaps before and after curing, bonding between blocks, and marks from the facing. Compare against a dressed-stone control.
